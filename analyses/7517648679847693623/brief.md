# BiteSight — TikTok teardown

35s · posted 19 Jun 2025 · 3.8M likes, 58.8K comments, 308.8K saves
https://www.tiktok.com/@realeater.flou/video/7517648679847693623

## What happens

Girl points at the camera: "My brother's gonna show you the app he built."
At 5s she hands off to him. He holds up his phone and scrolls real food
listings. "It's like TikTok meets DoorDash." Ends at 35s with no CTA.

## Why it worked

- Someone else does the hyping. He never sells his own app.
- One line explains the whole thing: TikTok meets DoorDash.
- One take. No cuts, no captions, no music. Feels like a real person, not an ad.
- Real restaurants and real prices on screen. Looks shipped, not mocked up.

## What's broken

- Dead 2.4s (3.5s–6s) while the camera swings over to him. Nothing said, nothing shown.
- Zero text on screen. Watch it muted and you learn nothing.
- The app name is only in the caption. Never said out loud, never on screen.
- No CTA. Nothing to tap.

## Comments

Top ones: "get a patent asap", "get this man on Shark Tank", "this is genius".
People are reacting to the idea, not the product. Nobody says they'd use it.

Two things worth noting:
- Ray William Johnson (verified, huge account) commented. 181K likes, 848 replies.
- People keep repeating the app name in the comments because the video never says it.

## What we copy

1. Get someone else to introduce our product.
2. Land one "X meets Y" line early.
3. One take, real data on screen, no edit.
4. Do what they didn't: name on screen + a CTA at the end.

## Open question

What's our version of "TikTok meets DoorDash"? Without that line this format
doesn't work.
